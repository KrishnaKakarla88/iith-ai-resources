# Category Policy Addendum: Toys

# KARTWAY CUSTOMER OPERATIONS POLICY HANDBOOK
## Document Ref: COP-ADD-TOY-004
### Category-Specific Policy Addendum: Toys & Games (CAT-TOY-09)
**Effective Date:** October 24, 2024  
**Policy Owner:** Julian Vance, VP of Customer Experience & Policy  
**Authorized By:** Marcus Thorne, Director of Quality Assurance  

---

### 1.0 Scope and Definition
*   **1.1** This addendum establishes the mandatory customer support protocols, return guidelines, restocking fees, and warranty parameters specifically for products listed under the "Toys & Games" super-category (ID: CAT-TOY-09) on the Kartway marketplace. 
*   **1.2** All transactions within CAT-TOY-09 are governed by this document. Where clauses in this addendum conflict with the general Kartway Standard Returns Policy (COP-04.1), this addendum shall take precedence.

### 2.0 Return Windows and Deadlines
*   **2.1 General Toy Return Window:** Notwithstanding the standard Kartway 30-day return policy, customers may initiate a return for eligible toys within **45 calendar days** from the date of confirmed carrier delivery.
*   **2.2 Semi-Perishable Toy Exception:** Products classified under sub-category CAT-TOY-PLAYDOUGH (including modeling clays, slimes, kinetic sands, and organic play doughs) are subject to a shortened return window of **10 calendar days** from the date of confirmed carrier delivery. Returns initiated on day 11 or later for this sub-category must be systematically blocked by support agents.

### 3.0 Condition Requirements and Packaging
*   **3.1 Original Packaging Mandate:** Original retail packaging is strictly mandatory for all toy returns. If the original retail box, cardboard backing, or blister pack is missing, the return request must be denied.
*   **3.2 Collectible Toys (Sub-category CAT-TOY-COLL):** Action figures, trading card boxes, and limited-edition board games must be returned in "Mint-in-Box" condition. 
    *   **3.2.1** If the factory shrink-wrap or circular clear wafer seals have been cut, broken, or peeled, the item is no longer considered collectible.
*   **3.3 Multi-Piece Board Games:** Board games containing more than **50 individual pieces** (cards, tokens, meeples, or dice) are ineligible for return once the inner factory-sealed plastic bags containing the components have been opened. Support agents must request photographic proof of the sealed inner components before issuing a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA).

### 4.0 Restocking Fees
*   **4.1 Opened Box Fee:** A flat **18% restocking fee** shall be deducted from the refund amount for any non-defective toy returned with broken outer factory seals but intact internal components.
*   **4.2 Collectible Depreciation Fee:** If a collectible toy (under clause 3.2) is returned with damaged packaging that does not compromise the toy itself (e.g., dented corners, creased cardbacks), a **40% depreciation fee** must be applied to the refund.
*   **4.3 Exemption:** Restocking fees are waived if the item is proven to have arrived damaged, or if the return is due to a verified shipping error by Kartway Fulfillment Services (KFS).

### 5.0 Hazardous and Perishable Material Handling
*   **5.1 Battery-Operated Toys:** 
    *   **5.1.1** Toys shipped with pre-installed or loose lithium-ion or button-cell batteries (e.g., CR2032) must be returned exclusively via ground transport. 
    *   **5.1.2** Support agents must generate Return Label "HAZ-TOY-B" which automatically restricts the shipment to ground freight. Air transport is strictly prohibited due to aviation safety regulations.
*   **5.2 Liquid and Chemical Toy Components:** 
    *   **5.2.1** Bubble solutions, science kit chemicals, toy cosmetics, and liquid paints must be completely sealed in their original, unpunctured vessels.
    *   **5.2.2** If any liquid seal is punctured, the item is classified as bio-hazardous and cannot be physically returned to a Kartway fulfillment center. 
    *   **5.2.3** In cases where a punctured liquid toy component is reported as damaged upon arrival, agents must instruct the buyer to dispose of the item locally and may issue a maximum **$15.00 gesture-of-goodwill credit** to the customer's Kartway balance, rather than processing a standard refund.

### 6.0 Warranty and Replacements
*   **6.1 Motorized and Ride-On Toys:** Ride-on toys (including electric cars, scooters, motorized hoverboards, and battery-powered dirt bikes) are backed by a **120-day Kartway parts-replacement warranty** starting from the delivery date. 
    *   **6.1.1** This warranty covers electrical failures, battery degradation below 60% capacity, and motor malfunctions. 
    *   **6.1.2** It does not cover cosmetic damage, tire wear, or damage resulting from exceeding the manufacturer's stated weight limit.
*   **6.2 General Toys Structural Warranty:** Non-electronic toys (e.g., plastic playsets, wooden blocks, construction sets) are covered by a **60-day structural warranty** against snapping, cracking, or joint failure under normal play conditions.
*   **6.3 Claims Processing:** All warranty claims under this section must be routed through the specialized Toy Warranty Desk (contact: `warranty-toys@kartway-ops.com`) for diagnostic review prior to parts dispatch or replacement unit authorization.
