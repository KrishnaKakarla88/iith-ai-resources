# Category Policy Addendum: Sports & Outdoors

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY HANDBOOK
## SECTION 14: CATEGORY-SPECIFIC ADDENDA
### SUB-SECTION 14.8: SPORTS & OUTDOORS POLICY

#### 14.8.1 Return Windows
*   **14.8.1.1 General Sports & Outdoors Return Window:** Notwithstanding the standard Kartway 30-day return policy outlined in Section 4.1, all items purchased within the Sports & Outdoors category qualify for an extended return window of **45 calendar days** from the confirmed date of delivery, subject to the conditions detailed in 14.8.2.
*   **14.8.1.2 Exception for Outdoor Electronics:** GPS units, fishfinders, action cameras, and fitness smartwatches are restricted to a shortened return window of **15 calendar days** from the date of delivery.
*   **14.8.1.3 Exception for Heavy/Oversized Fitness Equipment:** Treadmills, elliptical trainers, rowing machines, and home gym weight systems exceeding 100 lbs in shipping weight qualify for an extended satisfaction return window of **60 calendar days** from the date of delivery.

#### 14.8.2 Category-Specific Condition Requirements
*   **14.8.2.1 General Condition:** Items must be returned in clean, dry, and sanitary condition. Any item returned with visible mud, dirt, grass stains, or moisture will be rejected by the fulfillment center and returned to the buyer at their expense.
*   **14.8.2.2 Life Safety Gear:** Climbing harnesses, ropes, carabiners, helmets, and personal flotation devices (PFDs) are classified as Life Safety Gear. These items are strictly non-returnable if the original factory shrink-wrap, safety tags, or tamper-evident seals have been broken or altered in any manner.
*   **14.8.2.3 Technical Apparel & Wetsuits:** Wetsuits, drysuits, and high-altitude technical outerwear must be returned unworn with all original hangtags, plastic protective hygiene liners, and zipper guards fully intact.

#### 14.8.3 Original Packaging and Restocking Fees
*   **14.8.3.1 Original Packaging Threshold:** Original manufacturer packaging is mandatory for all returns where the purchase price of the item is **$150.00 USD or greater**. Returns of items valued at or above this threshold without original packaging will be refused.
*   **14.8.3.2 Wheeled Goods Restocking Fee:** A restocking fee of **12%** of the original purchase price will be assessed on all opened, non-defective returns in the "Wheeled Goods" sub-category, which includes but is not limited to: bicycles, electric bicycles (e-bikes), skateboards, and inline skates.
*   **14.8.3.3 Oversized Equipment Restocking Fee:** Any heavy/oversized fitness equipment (as defined in 14.8.1.3) returned without its original shipping pallet or structural crate will incur a flat restocking fee of **25%** of the original purchase price to cover the cost of specialized repalletization and freight handling.

#### 14.8.4 Hazardous and Perishable Handling Rules
*   **14.8.4.1 Pressurized and Flammable Materials:** Under no circumstances are customer returns permitted for pressurized gas canisters, including but not limited to: CO2 cartridges (used for paintball or bicycle tire inflation), propane canisters, butane canisters, and liquid camp stove fuel. These items are classified as Class 2 or Class 3 Hazardous Materials under DOT regulations and cannot be shipped via standard customer return labels. Customer service agents must issue a refund-without-return if a defect is verified by photo evidence.
*   **14.8.4.2 Camp Food and Survival Rations:** Freeze-dried meals, emergency water rations, and consumable energy gels must be unopened with the original vacuum seal fully intact. To be eligible for return, such items must have an expiration date at least **24 months** in the future from the date the return package is received at the Kartway processing facility.

#### 14.8.5 Warranty Period
*   **14.8.5.1 Structural Gear Warranty:** All structural outdoor gear, specifically limited to family camping tents, backpacking tents, kayaks, and stand-up paddleboards (SUPs), is covered by a **180-day limited marketplace warranty** administered by Kartway. This warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship, including seam failures, pole fractures, and hull delamination.
*   **14.8.5.2 Warranty Exclusions:** The 180-day warranty does not cover damage resulting from windstorms exceeding **35 miles per hour (mph)**, campfire ember burns, mold/mildew due to wet storage, or puncture damage sustained during recreational use on rocky terrain.
*   **14.8.5.3 Claims Process:** Warranty claims must be submitted to the Kartway Claims Department with a minimum of three (3) high-resolution photographs detailing the structural failure and a copy of the original transaction invoice.
