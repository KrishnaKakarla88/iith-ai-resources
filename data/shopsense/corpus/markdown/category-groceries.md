# Category Policy Addendum: Groceries

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY ADDENDUM: GROCERIES (DOCUMENT ID: POL-CAT-GR-04)

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2024  
**Version:** 4.2  
**Approved By:** Helena Vance, VP of Customer Operations  
**Supersedes:** POL-CAT-GR-03 (Dated January 15, 2023)  

---

### 1. SCOPE AND OVERRIDE AUTHORITY
*   **1.1** This document establishes the mandatory customer support protocols and merchant requirements for all transactions within the "Groceries" category on the Kartway marketplace.
*   **1.2** This policy explicitly supersedes the Kartway Standard Return Policy (POL-GEN-01), which mandates a standard 30-day return window. Where conflict arises between POL-GEN-01 and this addendum (POL-CAT-GR-04), this addendum shall govern.

---

### 2. RETURN WINDOWS BY SUB-CATEGORY
The standard 30-day return window is deactivated for all grocery items. Support agents must apply the following category-specific windows based on the delivery timestamp recorded by the carrier:

*   **2.1 Perishable Groceries (Sub-Category GR-PER):** 
    *   **2.1.1** Includes fresh produce, meat, seafood, dairy, frozen foods, and fresh bakery items.
    *   **2.1.2** The return window is exactly **48 hours** from the recorded delivery timestamp. No return requests or refund claims may be initiated after 48 hours.
*   **2.2 Non-Perishable Groceries (Sub-Category GR-NPER):**
    *   **2.2.1** Includes canned goods, dry pasta, spices, bottled beverages, shelf-stable condiments, and boxed snacks.
    *   **2.2.2** The return window is exactly **7 days** from the recorded delivery timestamp.

---

### 3. CONDITION AND PACKAGING REQUIREMENTS
*   **3.1 Non-Perishable Items (GR-NPER):**
    *   **3.1.1** Original packaging is 100% mandatory. 
    *   **3.1.2** To qualify for a return or refund under "Buyer's Remorse" reasons, the outer manufacturer packaging must be completely unopened, undamaged, and with all original factory safety seals fully intact.
    *   **3.1.3** Items returned with broken safety seals, punctured vacuum bags, or defaced nutritional labels must be rejected by the receiving warehouse, and no refund shall be issued.
*   **3.2 Perishable Items (GR-PER):**
    *   **3.2.1** Physical returns of perishable goods are strictly prohibited due to biological safety hazards.
    *   **3.2.2** For claims regarding spoilage, infestation, or transit damage, the customer must submit photographic evidence through the Kartway Customer Portal within **4 hours** of initiating the support ticket. 
    *   **3.2.3** The photograph must clearly display:
        *   The product expiration date or batch code.
        *   The specific defect (e.g., mold, bruising, ruptured packaging).
        *   The shipping label on the outer shipping carton.

---

### 4. RESTOCKING AND PROCESSING FEES
*   **4.1 Buyer's Remorse Returns (GR-NPER only):**
    *   **4.1.1** All voluntary, buyer-initiated returns for non-perishable grocery items (e.g., "ordered wrong flavor," "no longer wanted") are subject to a flat restocking fee of **$6.50** per SKU.
    *   **4.1.2** This fee is auto-deducted from the customer's refund balance during processing.
*   **4.2 Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items:**
    *   **4.2.1** No restocking fees or return shipping fees shall be assessed to the customer if the return is due to merchant error, carrier damage, or spoilage.
*   **4.3 Perishable Disposals:**
    *   **4.3.1** Since perishable items (GR-PER) are not physically returned, no restocking fee applies; however, a flat **$1.50** environmental disposal fee is charged to the selling merchant's ledger for any seller-fulfilled orders that result in a spoilage claim.

---

### 5. HAZARDOUS, LIQUID, AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
*   **5.1 Return-to-Sender Restrictions:**
    *   **5.1.1** Support agents must never issue a return shipping label for perishable items (GR-PER). All approved claims for perishables must be processed as "Refund Only - No Return Required" under disposal code **DISP-GROC-01**.
*   **5.2 Pressurized and Hazardous Aerosols (e.g., whipped cream, cooking sprays):**
    *   **5.2.1** These items are categorized as HAZMAT Class 9.
    *   **5.2.2** If a non-perishable return is authorized for a HAZMAT grocery item, the customer must ship the item via ground transport only. Shipping via air transport (Kartway Express Air) is strictly prohibited.
    *   **5.2.3** Agents must manually assign the return label to Kartway Ground Carrier partner "Velocity Express" (Partner ID: **VP-902**) to ensure regulatory compliance.
*   **5.3 Cold-Chain Integrity:**
    *   **5.3.1** In the event that a merchant is authorized to recall a bulk shipment of temperature-sensitive items, transit must maintain a temperature threshold below **4.0 degrees Celsius** (39.2 degrees Fahrenheit).
    *   **5.3.2** The merchant must provide temperature log data (via TempTale sensors) to Kartway Operations within **24 hours** of delivery to verify that the cold chain was not compromised.

---

### 6. WARRANTY AND FRESHNESS GUARANTEES
*   **6.1 Standard Warranty Exclusion:**
    *   **6.1.1** The standard Kartway 1-Year Limited Warranty (POL-WRN-09) is completely inapplicable to all items in the Grocery category. The warranty period for all grocery items is **0 days**.
*   **6.2 The Kartway Freshness Guarantee:**
    *   **6.2.1** In lieu of a warranty, Kartway guarantees that all perishables delivered to customers must have a minimum remaining shelf life of **5 days** past the delivery date.
    *   **6.2.2** If a customer receives a perishable product with a manufacturer expiration date printed on the packaging that is **4 days or fewer** from the date of delivery, the customer is entitled to a 100% refund under Policy Code **FG-RE-05**.
    *   **6.2.3** Claims under the Freshness Guarantee must be accompanied by a photo of the printed expiration date and must be filed within the 48-hour perishable return window.
