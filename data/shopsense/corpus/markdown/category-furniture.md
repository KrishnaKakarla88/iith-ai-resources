# Category Policy Addendum: Furniture

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY HANDBOOK
## SECTION 14: CATEGORY-SPECIFIC ADDENDUM – FURNITURE (FUR-001)

### 14.1 RETURN WINDOW AND ELIGIBILITY
* **14.1.1 Deviation from Standard Policy:** Notwithstanding the standard Kartway 30-day sitewide return policy, all transactions involving items categorized under Furniture (L2 Category Codes: FUR-001 through FUR-099) are subject to a shortened return window of **14 calendar days** commencing from the date of verified carrier delivery.
* **14.1.2 Initiation Deadline:** Returns must be initiated via the Kartway Customer Portal or through a Customer Operations Agent by 11:59 PM EST on the 14th calendar day post-delivery. No exceptions shall be granted for late registrations under any circumstances.
* **14.1.3 Named Exception (BedsByBensen):** Mattresses and bedframes manufactured by partner merchant **BedsByBensen LLC** are exempt from Section 14.1.1 and retain a **100-night** comfort trial return window, managed directly via the merchant’s dedicated dispatch team.

### 14.2 CATEGORY-SPECIFIC CONDITION REQUIREMENTS
* **14.2.1 Assembly Status:** Items must be returned in an unassembled state. Once assembly has been initiated (defined as the insertion of any screw, cam lock, dowel, or adhesive into pre-drilled holes), the item is ineligible for return under standard buyer's remorse guidelines.
* **14.2.2 Modification Exclusion:** Any physical modification, including but not limited to applying wood stain, fabric protectant sprays, or drilling additional holes, voids return eligibility immediately.
* **14.2.3 Inspection Protocol:** Customer Operations Agents must request three (3) distinct, high-resolution photographs of the disassembled item—specifically showing the corner joints, fabric surfaces, and hardware pack—prior to authorizing any return shipping label.

### 14.3 PACKAGING AND RESTOCKING FEES
* **14.3.1 Mandate of Original Packaging:** Original packaging is mandatory for all furniture returns. This includes the outer corrugated cardboard box, internal polystyrene corner protectors, and molded pulp trays. 
* **14.3.2 Restocking Fee Assessment:** A flat restocking fee of **15%** of the gross purchase price (prior to any promotional discounts or tax calculations) will be automatically deducted from the refund amount for any furniture item returned. This fee is waived only in cases of verified carrier transit damage documented within 48 hours of delivery.
* **14.3.3 Repackaging Fee Exception:** If a customer insists on returning an eligible item but has discarded the original cardboard container, a packaging supply fee of **$45.00** will be charged in addition to the 15% restocking fee. The customer must wait for a specialized "Kartway Box Kit Type-F" to be dispatched to their address before carrier pickup can be scheduled.

### 14.4 HAZARDOUS, HEAVY, AND SPECIAL HANDLING RULES
* **14.4.1 Weight Threshold for Freight Transport:** Any furniture item with a gross packaged weight exceeding **85 lbs** (38.5 kg) must not be routed through standard parcel networks (UPS, FedEx, or DHL). These returns must be routed through **Vanguard Logistics**, Kartway’s designated Less-Than-Truckload (LTL) freight partner.
* **14.4.2 Hazardous Chemical Additions:** Furniture items containing integrated fuel sources, such as built-in bio-ethanol fireplace inserts or chemically treated insect-resistant outdoor timber, require specialized handling:
    * **14.4.2.1 Fuel Drainage:** All liquid fuels must be completely drained from the reservoir by the customer prior to pickup.
    * **14.4.2.2 Form K-902 Declaration:** The customer must sign and tape a physical copy of **Form K-902 (Hazardous Substance Clearance Declaration)** to the exterior of the shipping crate. Without this form, Vanguard Logistics drivers are contractually obligated to refuse pickup.
* **14.4.3 Compressed Foam Items:** Foam mattresses, mattress toppers, and compressed beanbag chairs that have been unsealed from their vacuum-compressed shipping plastic cannot be repacked by the customer. The customer must not attempt to re-compress these items. These must be scheduled for disposal/recycle pickup via **EcoWaste Solutions** rather than being returned to a Kartway fulfillment center.

### 14.5 WARRANTY COVERAGE
* **14.5.1 Structural Warranty Duration:** All products classified under the Furniture category are backed by a mandatory **730-day (two-year)** limited structural warranty, which supersedes the standard Kartway 365-day general marketplace warranty.
* **14.5.2 Covered Defects:** This warranty covers structural failures including frame warping, weld cracking, joint separation, and premature sagging of internal spring coils under normal residential use conditions.
* **14.5.3 Excluded Damages:** The 730-day warranty explicitly excludes fabric pilling, fading due to direct sunlight exposure, water damage on non-outdoor rated items, and any damage resulting from exceeding the manufacturer-stated weight limit (defaulting to **250 lbs** per seated surface if no limit is specified by the manufacturer).
