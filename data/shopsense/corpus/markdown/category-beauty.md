# Category Policy Addendum: Beauty

# KARTWAY CUSTOMER OPERATIONS POLICY HANDBOOK
## ADDENDUM B: BEAUTY AND PERSONAL CARE CATEGORY (BPC-09)

### 1.0 SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS
* **1.1 Category Definition:** This addendum governs all transactions, returns, refunds, and warranties for products listed under the "Beauty & Personal Care" super-category, including cosmetics, skincare, haircare, fragrances, and beauty electronics.
* **1.2 Conflict of Terms:** Where provisions in this Addendum B conflict with the Kartway Standard Return Policy (KSRP-01), the provisions of this Addendum B shall take precedence for all Beauty and Personal Care items.

### 2.0 RETURN WINDOW
* **2.1 Standard Deviation:** Notwithstanding the standard Kartway 30-day return policy, the return window for all Beauty and Personal Care items is strictly limited to **14 calendar days** from the date of confirmed carrier delivery.
* **2.2 High-Value Beauty Electronics Exception:** Beauty electronics retailing for $250.00 USD or more must be registered for return within **7 calendar days** of delivery.

### 3.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND ORIGINAL PACKAGING
* **3.1 Hygienic Seal Requirement:** Any product that is applied directly to the skin, hair, nails, or mucous membranes (including but not limited to lipsticks, mascaras, foundations, and serums) is ineligible for return if the manufacturer's hygienic seal, shrink-wrap, or tamper-evident sticker has been broken, punctured, or removed.
* **3.2 Mandatory Packaging:** All returns must be executed in the original manufacturer’s packaging. The outer retail carton, protective plastic inserts, and any included clear-wrap must be present and undamaged.
* **3.3 Swatched or Tested Items:** Consumable cosmetics that have been "swatched," tested, or used even once are non-returnable under any circumstances, unless the customer initiates a claim under the Latent Defect Clause (see Clause 3.4).
* **3.4 Latent Defect Exception:** If a product causes an adverse dermatological reaction, a return may be authorized past the 14-day window, up to a maximum of **45 calendar days** from delivery. This exception requires the customer to upload a clear photograph of the reaction and requires written sign-off from the Customer Operations Tier 3 Lead, currently designated as **Sarah Jenkins-Vance**.

### 4.0 RESTOCKING FEES
* **4.1 Opened-Box Electronics:** Fully functional beauty electronics (such as hair dryers, straighteners, and facial steamers) returned with opened packaging will be subject to a flat **25% restocking fee**, calculated based on the original purchase price.
* **4.2 Consumable Items:** Opened but unused non-hygienically sealed consumable items (e.g., unsealed outer box but unused product bottle) will incur a **15% restocking fee**.
* **4.3 Restocking Fee Waiver:** Restocking fees are waived only if the return is due to a documented fulfillment error by the Kartway Fulfilment Center (KFC-East or KFC-West) or if the item is verified as Dead On Arrival (DOA) within **48 hours** of delivery.

### 5.0 HAZARDOUS AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
* **5.1 Flammable Liquids and Aerosols:** Products classified as hazardous materials due to alcohol or propellant content (including perfumes, colognes, aerosol dry shampoos, and nail polishes) cannot be returned via standard air mail.
    * **5.1.1 Shipping Method:** All flammable returns must be shipped via ground transport using the specialized Class 9 HazMat shipping label generated by the Kartway returns portal.
    * **5.1.2 Volume Limitation:** No individual container exceeding **150ml** in volume may be returned via the automated self-service portal. Any hazardous product exceeding **150ml** requires manual routing by the HazMat Compliance Desk.
* **5.2 Perishable and Expiry Controls:** Any topical skincare or cosmetic product with an active expiration date must have at least **180 days** of remaining shelf-life at the time the return is received at the Kartway processing facility. Products with fewer than **180 days** of remaining shelf-life will be rejected and disposed of as chemical waste, and no refund will be issued.

### 6.0 WARRANTY PERIODS
* **6.1 Beauty Electronics Warranty:** In deviation from Kartway’s standard 365-day electronics warranty, all plug-in or battery-operated beauty electronics carry a category-specific limited warranty of exactly **90 calendar days** from the purchase date.
* **6.2 Claims Process:** Warranty claims filed between day 91 and day 365 must be directed to the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) and are not the liability of Kartway.
* **6.3 Non-Electronic Items:** Consumable cosmetics, brushes, sponges, and topical creams carry no warranty post-delivery, save for transit damage claims filed within **48 hours** of delivery.
