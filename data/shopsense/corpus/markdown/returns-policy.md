# Kartway Returns and Refunds Policy

# KARTWAY CUSTOMER OPERATIONS POLICY HANDBOOK
## SECTION 2: RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY

### 2.1 Standard Return Window
*   **2.1.1** Except as specified in Clause 2.2, customers may initiate a return for any item purchased on Kartway within thirty (30) calendar days. 
*   **2.1.2** The 30-day window commences at 00:01 AM on the calendar day immediately following the delivery timestamp provided by the designated logistics carrier (e.g., FedEx, UPS, or Kartway Express).
*   **2.1.3** Return requests submitted at or after 12:00 AM on the 31st calendar day following delivery must be automatically rejected by the customer support system. No agent has the authority to override this system lockout unless authorized under the Special Exception Clause 2.1.4.
*   **2.1.4** **Special Exception (System Outage):** If a customer documents a system-wide Kartway platform outage that prevented return submission on Day 30, the Level 2 Support Lead (currently Sarah Jenkins) may authorize a manual override within 48 hours of service restoration.

### 2.2 Category-Specific Return Windows
The standard 30-day return window is superseded by category-specific rules for the following classifications:
*   **2.2.1 Electronics:** Items classified under the "Consumer Electronics" catalog (including but not limited to mobile phones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, television units, and audio accessories) must be returned within fifteen (15) calendar days from the carrier delivery timestamp.
*   **2.2.2 Apparel and Footwear:** Items classified under the "Apparel, Shoes & Fashion Accessories" catalog are eligible for an extended return window of forty-five (45) calendar days from the carrier delivery timestamp to accommodate sizing verification.
*   **2.2.3 Groceries and Perishables:** All items classified under "Fresh Grocery", "Pantry Staples", and "Perishable Goods" are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable once delivery is completed. 
    *   *2.2.3.1 Exception:* If a grocery item arrives damaged, expired past its printed expiry date, or is the incorrect item, agents must issue an instant refund to the customer's Kartway Wallet under the "Immediate Write-off Protocol" without requiring a physical return of the item.

### 2.3 Return Condition Requirements
To qualify for a 100% refund value, returned items must meet all of the following criteria upon physical inspection at the Kartway Fulfillment Center (KFC) in Toledo, Ohio:
*   **2.3.1** The item must be completely unused, unwashed, and free of any cosmetic, physical, or functional alterations.
*   **2.3.2** All original tags, security seals, manufacturer stickers, and brand packaging must remain intact, unbroken, and attached to the item in their original positions.
*   **2.3.3** The item must be returned with all original accessories, power adapters, instruction manuals, registration cards, and any "free gift" promotional items that were bundled with the primary purchase. Missing promotional items will result in their retail value being deducted from the final refund amount.

### 2.4 Return Shipping Cost Allocation
*   **2.4.1 Merchant/Platform Fault Returns:** Kartway will cover 100% of the return shipping costs if the return is necessitated by a platform or merchant error. This includes:
    *   *2.4.1.1* Receipt of a physically damaged or defective item.
    *   *2.4.1.2* Receipt of an item that materially differs from the product listing description.
    *   *2.4.1.3* Shipment of the incorrect size, color, or model by the merchant.
    *   *2.4.1.4* For all Merchant Fault returns, the agent must generate a prepaid UPS Ground shipping label via the Admin Console and email it to the customer.
*   **2.4.2 Buyer Remorse Returns:** The customer is solely responsible for return shipping costs if the return is due to "Buyer Remorse" reasons. This includes:
    *   *2.4.2.1* "Change of mind" or "No longer needed/wanted."
    *   *2.4.2.2* Accidental order placement.
    *   *2.4.2.3* Price finding elsewhere.
    *   *2.4.2.4* For all Buyer Remorse returns, Kartway will generate a return shipping label, and a flat-rate shipping fee of $6.50 USD will be automatically deducted from the customer's final refund total.
*   **2.4.3 Apparel Exception:** Notwithstanding Clause 2.4.2, change-of-mind returns for items under the "Apparel, Shoes & Fashion Accessories" category (Clause 2.2.2) qualify for free return shipping. No shipping fee deduction shall apply.

### 2.5 Refund Processing Timelines by Payment Method
Once a returned item is received, checked in, and approved by the KFC receiving team, the refund must be initiated within twenty-four (24) hours. The actual time for the funds to post to the customer's account is strictly governed by the payment method utilized during checkout:
*   **2.5.1 Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover):** Funds will post to the customer’s issuing bank account within five (5) to seven (7) business days from the refund initiation date. Agents must not expedite this timeline, as it is bound by external banking clearing cycles.
*   **2.5.2 Kartway Wallet (Store Credit):** Funds will be credited directly to the customer's digital Kartway Wallet within twenty-four (24) hours of return approval. This credit is non-transferable and cannot be liquidated into cash.
*   **2.5.3 Cash on Delivery (CoD):** For cash-based transactions, refunds will be processed via electronic funds transfer (EFT) to a validated bank account provided by the customer. This process requires manual verification by the Kartway Accounts Payable department and takes exactly ten (10) business days to post.

### 2.6 Partial-Refund Schedule for Opened Items
If a returned item does not meet the pristine condition requirements outlined in Clause 2.3, but is still deemed merchantable or salvageable by the KFC grading team, the agent must apply a partial refund according to the following strict schedule. Restocking fees are non-negotiable.

| Grading Tier | Item Condition Description | Restocking Fee | Refund Amount Issued to Customer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A** | Packaging seal is broken or shrink-wrap is removed, but the item itself is 100% pristine, unused, and all accessories are present. | 15% | **85% of original purchase price** |
| **Tier B** | Original retail packaging is damaged, torn, or discarded, but the item itself is pristine and all accessories are present. | 30% | **70% of original purchase price** |
| **Tier C** | Item is pristine, but non-essential accessories (e.g., auxiliary cables, mounting brackets, physical manuals) are missing. | 40% | **60% of original purchase price** |
| **Tier D** | Item shows minor cosmetic wear, light smudging, or evidence of brief test usage (less than 1 hour of cumulative operation). | 50% | **50% of original purchase price** |
| **Tier F** | Item is returned with major cosmetic damage, missing essential functional components (e.g., power cords, remote controls), or shows signs of heavy usage. | 100% (Rejected) | **0% (No refund).** The item will be disposed of, or mailed back to the customer at a flat shipping charge of $12.00 USD. |
