### 1.0 SCOPE AND APPLICATION

**1.1 Covered Products:** This policy addendum applies strictly to all transactions, returns, and disputes involving items listed under the "Electronics" root category (System ID: CAT-200), including but not limited to mobile devices, computers, smart home devices, audio equipment, wearable technology, and gaming consoles.

**1.2 Precedence:** Where provisions in this addendum conflict with the Kartway Standard 30-Day Return Policy (COP-GEN-001), this Electronics Addendum shall take absolute precedence.

---

### 2.0 RETURN WINDOW AND DEADLINES

**2.1 Return Window:** Notwithstanding the standard Kartway 30-day return policy, the return window for all CAT-200 Electronics is strictly limited to **14 calendar days** commencing from the timestamp of confirmed delivery as recorded by the carrier.

**2.2 Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Exception:** Items that are non-functional upon receipt must be reported to Kartway Customer Support within **48 hours** of confirmed delivery. Reports received after 48 hours will not qualify for the expedited DOA replacement protocol and must proceed through the standard 14-day return assessment.

**2.3 Late Returns:** Any return postmarked or dropped off at an authorized carrier location after the 14-calendar-day window has expired must be systematically rejected by support agents, with no option for administrative override.

---

### 3.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND ORIGINAL PACKAGING

**3.1 Serial Number Verification:** No refund or replacement shall be processed unless the serial number on the returned physical item matches the outbound serial number scanned at the fulfillment center and logged in the "KartGuard" inventory system. 

**3.2 Original Packaging Requirement:** Original packaging is mandatory for all non-defective (buyer's remorse) returns. The item must be returned with:
*   The original retail box, undamaged and containing the intact UPC barcode.
*   All original internal structural packaging (molded cardboard, plastic trays, foam inserts).
*   All original accessories, including power bricks, charging cables, remote controls, and documentation/manuals.

**3.3 Defective Returns Packaging:** If an item is validated as defective, original packaging is preferred but not mandatory. However, the buyer must pack the item in protective bubble wrap or equivalent anti-static packaging to prevent transit damage.

**3.4 Refusal of Damaged Returns:** If an item is returned damaged due to improper packaging by the customer, support agents must deny the refund and instruct the logistics team to discard the item or ship it back to the customer at the customer’s expense.

---

### 4.0 RESTOCKING FEES AND DEDUCTIONS

**4.1 Open-Box Restocking Fee:** A flat restocking fee of **15% of the gross purchase price** shall be deducted from the buyer's refund for any non-defective electronics item returned with opened or unsealed original retail packaging.

**4.2 Sealed-Item Exception:** The 15% restocking fee shall be waived only if the item is returned completely sealed in its original factory shrink-wrap, with all security stickers and holographic seals completely unbroken.

**4.3 Missing Accessories Deduction:** If a return is accepted with missing non-essential accessories (e.g., a missing USB-C cable or user manual), a flat deduction of **$25.00 USD** or **10% of the item's purchase price** (whichever is greater) shall be applied to the refund, in addition to any applicable restocking fees under Clause 4.1. This deduction must be authorized by the Technical Inspection Depot (TID) supervisor, Harrison Finch.

---

### 5.0 HAZARDOUS MATERIALS & LITHIUM-ION BATTERIES

**5.1 High-Capacity Battery Restrictions:** Any electronic device containing a lithium-ion battery with a capacity exceeding **100 Watt-hours (Wh)** (including but not limited to electric skateboards, high-capacity power stations, and select high-performance laptops) cannot be returned via standard pre-paid shipping labels. 
*   Buyers must coordinate return shipping exclusively through Kartway’s certified hazardous materials logistics partner, **VoltLogistics**.
*   The buyer is responsible for a **$45.00 USD** hazardous materials handling surcharge, which will be deducted from the final refund.

**5.2 Damaged or Swollen Batteries:** Under no circumstances may a buyer ship any electronic device with a physically damaged, swollen, punctured, or leaking battery. 
*   Support agents must immediately issue a "Dispose in Place" directive to the customer.
*   To secure a refund for such items, the customer must provide a certified receipt of disposal from a licensed municipal e-waste recycling facility within **7 calendar days** of the directive.
*   The case must be escalated to the hazardous safety team lead, Clara Higgins, for manual refund approval.

---

### 6.0 WARRANTY AND POST-PURCHASE SUPPORT

**6.1 Kartway Certified Refurbished Warranty:** All items purchased under the "Kartway Certified Refurbished" sub-category carry an exclusive **180-day parts and labor warranty** administered directly by our authorized repair partner, **FixTek Solutions**. This warranty begins on day 15 post-delivery (immediately following the expiration of the 14-day return window).

**6.2 New-in-Box Warranty Pass-Through:** For new-in-box items, Kartway provides a **90-day marketplace warranty** against mechanical and electrical defects. Beyond 90 days, the buyer must seek resolution directly through the manufacturer’s warranty program. Kartway support agents are prohibited from issuing refunds, replacements, or store credit for new items after day 90.

**6.3 Warranty Claim Exclusions:** The warranties outlined in Clauses 6.1 and 6.2 strictly exclude coverage for:
*   Accidental damage (liquid spills, cracked screens, drops).
*   Software modification (rooting, jailbreaking, custom firmware installation).
*   Use of non-standard charging equipment exceeding manufacturer voltage specifications.
